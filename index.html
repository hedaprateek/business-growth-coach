A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is this coaching right for my business?+

Coaching is a good starting point if you run a business and want help with priorities, systems, or your next stage of growth. In our first conversation, we’ll discuss your situation and decide whether this approach fits what you need.

What happens in the first conversation?+

We’ll talk about your business, what you want to change, and your biggest current challenge. You can ask questions about the coaching approach and discuss possible next steps before deciding to work together.

Will you do the work for me?+

Coaching helps you make decisions and put them into action. We’ll develop your plan together, but you and your team stay responsible for running the business and implementing changes.

How long does coaching take?+

That depends on your goals and the support you need. We’ll agree on a useful rhythm and time frame together, with regular reviews so the work stays relevant.

How much does it cost?+

We’ll discuss the scope, session frequency, and fees after understanding your needs. You’ll have a clear proposal to consider before making a commitment.

Can we work together remotely?+

Yes, coaching can take place over a video call. We’ll agree on a format and schedule that fit your business.
